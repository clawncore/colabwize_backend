# ColabWize — Billing & Plans

## Payment Processor
LemonSqueezy (processes Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal)

## Subscription Plans

- 1 Team Workspace, 2 collaborators
- 3 rephrases/month (1 variant)
- Preview certificate (watermarked)
- 3 document scans/month, 20k character max
- 5 AI chat messages
- 25 paper searches
- 5MB file size limit
- Basic support

### Plus — $5.99/month ($57.50/year — 20% savings)
- 5 Team Workspaces, 10 collaborators each
- Chat with PDF, Unlimited PDF chats
- 25 rephrases (up to 3 variants)
- 50MB file size limit
- Citation confidence analysis
- Version history, Collaboration logs
- Professional certificate generation
- Export without watermark
- Email support

### Premium — $12.99/month ($124.70/year — 20% savings)
- Unlimited workspaces and collaborators
- Advanced analytics
- 100 citation audits/month (200k chars/doc)
- Chat with My Projects, Unlimited
- Advanced Research, Insight Map, Research Gaps, Search Alerts
- Draft Comparison
- 100MB file size, 30 PDFs chat
- Institution-grade certificates
- Priority scanning
- Priority support

## Credit Packs (One-Time Purchase, Never Expire)
- 1 credit = 1,000 words processed
- Starter: 5 credits — $1.99
- Professional: 25 credits — $6.99
- Enterprise: 50 credits — $12.99

## How Credits Work
- Credits are used when plan quota is exhausted
- Must opt in to auto-use credits in Settings > Billing
- Credits never expire
- 1 credit = 1,000 words processed

## Refund Policy
- Full refund within 7 days of purchase
- Pro-rated refund for annual plans
- Email billing@colabwize.com with account email and reason
- Refunds process within 5-7 business days

## How to Upgrade
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Upgrade Plan"
3. Choose monthly or yearly billing
4. Enter payment details
5. Instant access to new features

## How to Cancel
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Cancel Subscription"
3. Confirmation email sent
4. Access continues until end of current billing period
5. Data retained for 30 days after cancellation

## How to Update Payment Method
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Manage Subscription" (opens LemonSqueezy portal)
3. Update card details
4. Changes apply immediately

## Invoices

- Also emailed to account email on each billing cycle
- Contact billing@colabwize.com for custom invoicing
