# ColabWize — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is ColabWize?
ColabWize is an academic writing integrity platform. It helps students and researchers verify citations, prove authorship, detect AI-generated content, check for plagiarism, and collaborate on documents in real-time.

### Is ColabWize free?
Yes — the Free plan includes basic features with monthly limits. Plus ($5.99/mo) and Premium ($12.99/mo) plans unlock more features and higher limits.

### What file formats are supported?
PDF and DOCX for documents. BibTeX and RIS for references. Export to PDF, DOCX, BibTeX, RIS.

### Is my data safe?
Yes. We use TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 at rest, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and quarterly penetration testing. Your documents are never shared or sold. See our Privacy Policy at colabwize.com/legal/privacy.

### Does ColabWize work on mobile?
The platform is optimized for desktop browsers. Mobile access is available but some features work best on larger screens.

## Citations

### How does citation verification work?
We check your citations against CrossRef, PubMed, ArXiv, and OpenAlex databases. We verify: author names, paper titles, publication years, journal names, DOIs, and whether the paper has been retracted.

### What citation styles are supported?
APA, MLA, Chicago, and general academic formatting. We're adding more styles regularly.

### My citation shows low confidence — why?
Common reasons: very recent paper not yet in databases, non-English source, unusual formatting, or the citation doesn't match any known publication. Try reformatting the citation or checking the original source.

### Can I verify citations in a PDF?
Yes. Upload the PDF to Citation Audit and we'll extract and verify all citations automatically.

## Authorship

### What is an authorship certificate?
A PDF certificate that proves you wrote your document. It includes: timestamped edit history, hours logged, total revisions, QR-coded verification link. Anyone can verify it at colabwize.com/verify/:id.

### How do I generate a certificate?
1. Write your document using ColabWize (time tracking must be enabled)
2. Go to Dashboard > Certificate
3. Select document and certificate type
4. Download PDF (5-10 seconds to generate)

### Can I verify someone else's certificate?
Yes. Scan the QR code or visit the verification URL printed on the certificate. No login required.

## AI Detection

### How does AI detection work?
We use GPTZero to scan text and classify each sentence as human, likely_human, likely_ai, or ai. The overall document is classified as human, mixed, or ai.

### Is AI detection 100% accurate?
No — no AI detector is perfect. Use it as one data point alongside other evidence. False positives can occur, especially with heavily edited AI text.

### Can I bypass AI detection by paraphrasing?
We don't recommend attempting to disguise AI-generated content as human-written. Our "Humanization" tool is designed to improve writing quality, not to evade detection.

## Collaboration

### How many people can collaborate on a document?
Depends on your plan: Free (2 per workspace), Plus (10 per workspace), Premium (unlimited).

### Can I see who edited what?
Yes. Version history tracks all changes with timestamps and user names. Available on Plus and Premium plans.

### Does real-time collaboration work offline?
No. Real-time sync requires an internet connection. Your work is saved locally and syncs when you reconnect.

## Technical

### What browsers are supported?
Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions). Opera Mini and very old browsers are not supported.

### Why should I disable Grammarly?
Grammarly's browser extension conflicts with our Tiptap editor, causing formatting issues and cursor jumping. Disable it while using ColabWize.

### The app is running slowly — what can I do?
1. Clear browser cache
2. Close unnecessary tabs
3. Disable browser extensions
4. Use Chrome for best performance
5. Check internet connection

### Can I use ColabWize without internet?
No. The platform requires an internet connection for all features including real-time collaboration, AI tools, and citation verification.
